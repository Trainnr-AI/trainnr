# Getting help

- **Questions, ideas, show and tell:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Trainnr-AI/trainnr/discussions).
- **A bug, a feature request, a robot or format to support:** [open an issue](https://github.com/Trainnr-AI/trainnr/issues/new/choose); the forms ask for what a maintainer needs to act.
- **A security problem:** never in an issue; follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
- **Code of conduct concerns:** privately, to the contact under *Enforcement* in [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md); never in an issue.

What to expect: trainnr has one maintainer today. New issues and
discussions are triaged within three business days, best effort;
security reports follow the timeline in `SECURITY.md`. A minimal,
reproducible report (the tool call or command, what you expected, what
happened, the version) is the fastest way to a fix.
